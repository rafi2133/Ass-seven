# Ass-Seven - Friend Relationship Manager

A comprehensive web application to help you track, nurture, and maintain meaningful connections with your friends. Keep track of your interactions, set relationship goals, and never lose touch with the people who matter most.

## 🚀 Live Demo

https://ass-seven.netlify.app/

## 📋 Description

Ass-Seven is a relationship management tool designed to help users maintain consistent communication with their friends. The app allows you to track your friends, log interactions (calls, texts, video calls), set relationship goals, and visualize your communication patterns through interactive charts. With a clean and intuitive interface, you can easily manage your social connections and ensure you're staying in touch regularly.

## ✨ Key Features

### 1. Friend Management
- View all your friends in a responsive grid layout
- Each friend card displays profile picture, name, last contact days, tags, and status
- Click on any friend to view detailed information including bio, email, and interaction history
- Track friend status with color-coded badges (On Track, Almost Due, Overdue)

### 2. Interaction Tracking
- Log three types of interactions: Calls, Texts, and Video Calls
- Each interaction automatically records the current date and time
- View all interactions in a chronological timeline
- Filter timeline by interaction type (Call/Text/Video)
- Prevent duplicate interactions with smart duplicate detection

### 3. Statistics & Analytics
- Visual representation of your interactions through an interactive pie chart
- Real-time statistics showing distribution of communication methods
- Track your relationship goals (e.g., connect every 14 days)
- Monitor days since last contact for each friend
- Quick check-in buttons for easy interaction logging
